Just a reminder which helps me doing responsive design’s [Media Query](http://www.w3.org/TR/css3-mediaqueries/).

-   @media (max-width: 767px)
-   @media (max-width: 480px) 
-   @media (min-width: 768px) and (max-width: 979px)
-   @media (min-width: 980px)
-   @media (min-width: 1200px)

Note: **the order above matters. Changing it lets later rules override earlier CSS.**
